historical and cultural terms

a
ashoka edicts
rock carvings of emperor ashoka containing moral and administrative directives promoting buddhism and dharma
aryabhata
gupta period mathematician who developed zero and decimal system
arya samaj
social reform movement founded by dayananda saraswati in 19th century
ashram
hermitage or monastery where spiritual and intellectual learning occurs
b
brahmins
highest caste in varna system, traditionally priests and scholars
brahmo samaj
social reform movement founded by rajaram mohan roy
buddhism
religion and philosophy founded by siddhartha gautama based on dharma and enlightenment
bhakti
devotional movement emphasizing personal connection with deity
c
caste system
social hierarchy system dividing society into hereditary classes
chola empire
south indian maritime and military power controlling spice trade routes
chandragupta maurya
founder of mauryan empire with centralized administration
d
delhi sultanate
islamic rule in north india preceding mughal empire
dharma
cosmic law and duty, ethical and moral principles in hinduism and buddhism
dynasty
succession of rulers from same family or lineage
din-i-ilahi
akbar's attempt to synthesize hinduism and islam
e
empire
large political unit with central authority controlling multiple territories and peoples
edict
official proclamation or decree by ruler
g
gupta empire
golden age of classical indian culture and scientific advancement
gopuram
ornamental tower gate at entrance of south indian temple
guru
teacher or spiritual master in hindu and sikh traditions
h
harappa
indus valley civilization site in punjab
hinduism
major religion originating in india based on vedas and diverse philosophical traditions
i
indus valley civilization
ancient urban civilization in northwestern india with sophisticated planning and undeciphered script
independence
freedom from colonial rule achieved by india in 1947
j
jainism
religion emphasizing non-violence and karma philosophy
jizya
tax levied on non-muslims in islamic states, abolished by akbar
k
karma
law of cause and effect in hindu and buddhist philosophy
kalidasa
greatest classical sanskrit poet of gupta period
kautilya
advisor to chandragupta maurya who wrote arthashastra on statecraft
m
mauryan empire
first major empire in ancient india under chandragupta and ashoka
mohenjo-daro
major indus valley civilization city with advanced urban planning
mughal empire
indo-islamic dynasty ruling most of india for over 300 years
monastery
religious community where monks practice spiritual discipline
n
nirvana
state of enlightenment and liberation in buddhism
p
partition
division of british india into india and pakistan in 1947
pataliputra
capital of mauryan and gupta empires
r
rajput
warrior caste in northern india ruling various kingdoms
raja raja chola i
greatest chola emperor who expanded empire and built brihadisvara temple
s
stupa
dome-shaped buddhist shrine housing relics of buddha or saints
shah jahan
mughal emperor who commissioned taj mahal
sati
practice of widow self-immolation, opposed by social reformers
t
taj mahal
monument built by shah jahan for wife mumtaz mahal, architectural masterpiece
thanjavur
capital of chola empire
v
vedas
ancient sacred texts of hinduism including rigveda, yajurveda, samaveda, atharvaveda
vedic period
era in which vedas were composed with arrival of indo-aryans
vijayanagara empire
major south indian empire known for administrative excellence and temple architecture
