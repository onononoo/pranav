welcome

this is the indian history database. it contains information about the major periods, empires, dynasties, and historical figures of the indian subcontinent.

database contents

  • empires and kingdoms - major ruling powers across history
  • dynasties - detailed information about ruling families
  • historical figures - important people who shaped history
  • archaeological sites - locations of historical importance
  • timeline - chronological view of events
  • geographical regions - regional variations and features
  • architecture - temples, forts, and monuments
  • art and culture - dance, music, literature, visual arts
  • religion and philosophy - spiritual traditions and beliefs
  • military history - battles, warfare, military organization
  • glossary - terms and definitions

periods covered

period years description
ancient india 3300 bce - 800 ce indus valley, vedic, mauryan, gupta
medieval india 800 ce - 1526 ce rajput kingdoms, delhi sultanate, south india
colonial period 1600 ce - 1947 ce european expansion, british rule
modern india 1947 ce - present independence, republic, development

quick facts

  • the indus valley civilization flourished around 3300 bce with advanced cities
  • the vedic period established the foundations of hinduism
  • the mauryan empire under ashoka spread buddhism across asia
  • the gupta empire marked the golden age of classical indian culture
  • the mughal empire created lasting architectural masterpieces
  • india gained independence in 1947 and became a democratic republic in 1950
