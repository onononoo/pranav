indus valley civilization

the indus valley civilization flourished around 3300 bce in the northwestern region of the indian subcontinent. major cities: harappa, mohenjo-daro. advanced urban planning with drainage systems and standardized weights. undeciphered script on seals.

vedic period

period marked by arrival of indo-aryan peoples. composition of four vedas: rigveda, yajurveda, samaveda, atharvaveda. establishment of caste system and vedic rituals.

mauryan empire

founded 322 bce by chandragupta maurya. peak under emperor ashoka. spread of buddhism. extensive military campaigns. rock edicts of ashoka found across empire.

gupta empire

golden age of classical india. advances in mathematics including zero and decimal system. classical sanskrit literature. kalidasa, aryabhata. temple architecture and sculpture.

rajput kingdoms

warrior-ruled kingdoms in north india. mewar, marwar, amber regions. architectural achievements in forts and palaces. resistance against invasions.

delhi sultanate

established by qutubuddin aibak. multiple dynasties ruled delhi for centuries. tughlak, sayyid, lodi dynasties. islamic architecture and administration.
