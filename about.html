about this database

this is a comprehensive database documenting the history of the indian subcontinent. it covers major periods from the earliest civilizations to the present era, highlighting key figures, empires, and cultural achievements.

scope

the database includes information about:

  • ancient civilizations and vedic period
  • major empires including mauryan, gupta, mughal
  • south indian kingdoms and regional powers
  • colonial period and independence struggle
  • modern india and contemporary developments

purpose

this project aims to provide accessible information about indian history for students, researchers, and enthusiasts. the content is organized chronologically and thematically for easy navigation and understanding.
