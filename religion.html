religious and philosophical traditions

hinduism

vedic religion

  • vedas - oldest scriptures (rigveda, yajurveda, samaveda, atharvaveda)
  • vedic rituals and sacrifices (yajna)
  • fire worship and cosmic order (rta)
  • vedic deities - indra, agni, soma, varuna
  • foundation of later hindu philosophy

upanishadic philosophy

  • exploration of brahman (ultimate reality)
  • atman-brahman identity (tat tvam asi)
  • karma and rebirth cycles
  • pursuit of moksha (liberation)
  • teachers and spiritual lineages

later hinduism

  • puranic mythology - worship of krishna, shiva, devi
  • temple worship and devotion
  • bhakti movement - emotional devotion
  • regional variations in practice
  • caste system and social order

buddhism

origins

  • gautama buddha (500 bce) - founder
  • four noble truths - suffering and its cessation
  • eightfold path - ethical conduct
  • monastic order - sangha
  • early spread through ashoka's patronage

schools of buddhism

  • theravada - strict adherence to early texts
  • mahayana - expanded pantheon and salvation paths
  • vajrayana - tantric practices and rituals
  • regional variations in tibet, china, japan

major pilgrimage sites

  • bodh gaya - site of buddha's enlightenment
  • sarnath - location of first sermon
  • kushinagar - buddha's parinirvana
  • lumbini - buddha's birthplace

jainism

principles

  • mahavira (599-527 bce) - last tirthankara
  • ahimsa (non-violence) - core principle
  • karma and soul purification
  • ascetic practices and renunciation
  • strict vegetarianism and monastic life

philosophy

  • anekantavada - many-sidedness of truth
  • syadvada - conditional nature of statements
  • karma as material substance
  • jiva (soul) and ajiva (non-soul) dualism

sikhism

  • guru nanak (1469-1539) - founder
  • monotheistic faith with egalitarian principles
  • scripture: guru granth sahib
  • gurdwaras - community centers and temples
  • distinctly turbans and unshorn hair

islam in india

arrival and spread

  • arab traders in 7th century
  • delhi sultanate and mughal empire
  • sufi saints and devotional Islam
  • syncretic practices in certain regions
  • major religious centers: delhi, agra

contributions

  • urdu language development
  • indo-islamic architecture
  • persian cultural influences
  • qawwali devotional music
  • theological and mystical writings

philosophical schools

religious synthesis

bhakti movement

  • devotional approach to god
  • saints like kabir, ravi das, tulsidas
  • songs and poetry as spiritual practice
  • social inclusivity across castes
  • influence on later reform movements

sufi mysticism

  • mystical islam emphasizing inner experience
  • sufi orders and spiritual lineages
  • qawwali music and devotional practices
  • synthesis with hindu devotional traditions

interfaith relations

  • hindu-muslim cultural exchanges
  • composite culture in certain periods
  • regional variations in coexistence
  • conflict and harmony across history

spiritual practices

  • meditation and contemplation
  • ritual worship and ceremonies
  • pilgrimage to sacred sites
  • yoga and physical discipline
  • guru-student spiritual transmission
  • festivals and celebrations
