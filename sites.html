site details

taj mahal - agra, uttar pradesh

period: mughal (17th century)

builder: shah jahan

purpose: monument to wife mumtaz mahal

architecture: indo-islamic fusion with marble inlay work, calligraphy, symmetrical gardens

significance: world heritage site, architectural masterpiece, symbol of mughal grandeur, one of wonders of world

mohenjo-daro - sindh

period: indus valley civilization (3300 bce)

significance: ancient city ruins showing advanced urban planning, drainage systems, standardized weights

features: great bath, granary, residential areas, undeciphered script on seals

importance: demonstrates sophistication of ancient indus civilization

brihadisvara temple - thanjavur

period: chola (11th century)

builder: raja raja chola i

architecture: granite construction, gopuram tower, intricate carvings

significance: masterpiece of chola temple architecture, bronze sculptures, artistic excellence

ajanta caves - maharashtra

period: ancient (1st-5th century ce)

significance: rock-cut buddhist temples and monasteries with murals

features: sculptures, paintings depicting buddhist stories, architectural planning

importance: example of ancient indian art and buddhist culture

red fort - delhi

period: mughal (17th century)

builder: shah jahan

purpose: imperial palace and fortress

significance: red sandstone walls, palace halls, lagoon gardens, architectural grandeur

khajuraho temples - madhya pradesh

period: medieval (10th-11th century)

builder: chandela dynasty

architecture: sandstone construction with intricate carvings of deities and scenes

significance: world heritage site, artistic excellence, temple architecture masterpiece
