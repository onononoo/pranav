ancient india timeline

3300 bce

indus valley civilization

flourishing of one of the world's earliest urban civilizations with cities like harappa and mohenjo-daro

1500-1200 bce

vedic period begins

arrival of indo-aryan peoples and composition of the rigveda, oldest vedic text

600 bce

birth of major religions

emergence of buddhism through siddhartha gautama and other philosophical movements

322 bce

mauryan empire founded

chandragupta maurya establishes centralized empire with capital at pataliputra

268-232 bce

ashoka's reign

emperor ashoka converts to buddhism and spreads dharma through rock edicts across the empire

320 ce

gupta empire founded

chandragupta i establishes the gupta dynasty, beginning india's golden age

375-415 ce

chandragupta ii's reign

golden age continues with advances in mathematics, astronomy, literature and arts

medieval india timeline

850 ce

chola dynasty rise

chola empire emerges as major maritime and military power in south india

985-1014 ce

raja raja chola i

chola empire reaches peak with great expansion and construction of brihadisvara temple

1191 ce

delhi sultanate established

qutubuddin aibak founds delhi sultanate, marking beginning of islamic rule in north india

1336 ce

vijayanagara empire founded

vijayanagara emerges as major south indian power with sophisticated administration

1526 ce

mughal empire founded

babur defeats ibrahim lodi at panipat and establishes mughal dynasty

1556-1605 ce

akbar the great's reign

mughal empire reaches zenith with administrative reforms and religious tolerance

1632-1653 ce

taj mahal construction

shah jahan commissions taj mahal as monument to his wife mumtaz mahal

colonial period timeline

1600 ce

british east india company founded

company begins trading operations in india with establishment of trading posts

1757 ce

battle of plassey

british victory marks effective control over bengal and beginning of territorial expansion

1858 ce

india becomes crown colony

british crown assumes direct control following suppression of indian rebellion

1885 ce

indian national congress formed

establishment of political organization that would lead independence movement

1920s-1940s

independence struggle intensifies

non-cooperation movement, civil disobedience, and quit india movement challenge british rule

modern india timeline

1947 ce

independence achieved

india gains independence from british rule with partition creating pakistan

1950 ce

republic proclaimed

india becomes sovereign democratic republic with adoption of constitution

1960s-1970s

nation building era

five-year plans, industrialization, and infrastructure development shape modern india

1991 ce

economic liberalization

india opens economy to global markets, beginning technological advancement

2000s-present

global emergence

india becomes technological powerhouse, space explorer, and major global player
