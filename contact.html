faq

how accurate is the database?

the database is compiled from scholarly historical research and published sources. however, historical interpretation can vary. we encourage feedback on any inaccuracies.

can i use this for academic work?

yes, this database is suitable for academic reference. please cite appropriately when using information.

how often is the database updated?

the database is updated periodically with new research and information. check the main page for latest updates.

can i contribute content?

we welcome contributions from researchers and historians. please contact us with your proposals and credentials.
